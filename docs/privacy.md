# Privacy Summary

This page is a public-facing summary for users evaluating the Windows installer and online features.
If a fuller legal policy is published on the website, that website version controls.

## What this product may process

### Local Office activity
When you run local automation commands, the add-in works with the active document, workbook, or presentation content needed to complete the action on your machine.

### Explicit AI-assisted actions
If you trigger translation, summarization, generation, command building, or similar AI features, the product may process:

- selected text or document fragments you choose to use
- prompts or instructions you enter
- limited context required to complete the request

Do not submit confidential, unlawful, infringing, or highly sensitive content to AI features.

### Account and community data
If you sign in or use community features, the service may store account identifiers, display name, contact fields you provide, and public posts/replies you publish.

### Diagnostics and feedback
If you submit feedback or logs, you may upload:

- product version
- Office / Windows version
- error messages
- screenshots
- log or dump fragments you attach

Redact private business data before upload.

### Update and security related data
Version checks and abuse-prevention mechanisms may use coarse technical signals such as product version, request time, IP address in server logs, and non-reversible device deduplication identifiers. Raw hardware serial numbers should not be uploaded.

## What we aim not to do

- silently publish your private documents to a public website
- require public GitHub issues for private incident data
- use diagnostics as an advertising profile system

## Cookies / website locale

The public website may store a locale preference cookie so Chinese / English browsing can persist.

## Third-party services

Current public builds should clearly disclose whether payments, third-party login, analytics SDKs, or external model providers are enabled.
Before relying on this summary for compliance review, verify the website policy for the exact release channel you use.

## Your choices

- Use local features without invoking AI actions when possible.
- Avoid uploading sensitive attachments in feedback.
- Request account correction/deletion through the support channel when account features are enabled.
